# The admin app and notifications

How to put the Zain's Admin app on your phone and get a notification
when an order or message comes in. It takes about two minutes.

## iPhone

You need iOS 16.4 or later (Settings → General → About → iOS Version).

1. Open **Safari** (not Chrome) and go to the admin address you use to
   sign in, for example `https://cms.zainstreat.com`.
2. Tap the **Share** button (the square with an arrow pointing up).
3. Scroll down and tap **Add to Home Screen**, then **Add**.
4. Close Safari and open **Zain's Admin** from your Home Screen. Sign in
   if it asks.
5. Tap **Settings**, then **Turn on notifications**, then **Allow**.
6. Tap **Send test notification**. A "Test notification" should appear
   within a few seconds.

On iPhone, notifications only work from the Home Screen app, not from a
Safari tab.

## Android

1. Open **Chrome** and go to the admin address, then sign in.
2. Go to **Settings** and tap **Install app** (or Chrome's ⋮ menu →
   **Install app**).
3. Open **Zain's Admin** from your home screen.
4. Tap **Settings**, then **Turn on notifications**, then **Allow**.
5. Tap **Send test notification** to check it works.

## Computer

In Chrome or Edge, open the admin, go to **Settings** and tap **Turn on
notifications**. Installing the app is optional on a computer.

## What you'll get

| Notification | When | Tapping it opens |
|---|---|---|
| New order ZTM-… | A customer places an order | The order |
| Quote #… accepted | A customer accepts a catering quote (this creates an order) | The new order |
| Payment claimed — ZTM-… | A customer says they've paid | The order |
| New catering enquiry | Someone sends a catering enquiry | The enquiry |
| Quote #… declined | A customer declines a quote | The enquiry |
| New message from … | Someone uses the contact form | The message |

Notifications show the customer's name. To hide them on a locked phone,
use iPhone Settings → Notifications → Show Previews → **When Unlocked**.

The number on the app icon is how many orders are waiting for payment
or for you to confirm a payment.

You still get the same emails as before. Notifications are an extra.

## If notifications stop

- Open the app, go to **Settings** and check it says "Notifications are
  on for this device". If not, tap **Turn on notifications**.
- If it says notifications are **blocked**, allow them again:
  iPhone Settings → Notifications → Zain's Admin, or on Android, press and
  hold the app icon → App info → Notifications.
- The **Devices** list shows every phone and computer that gets
  notifications. **Remove** any you no longer use.
